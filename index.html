Compatibility notes

This first build imports VRM/GLB and transfers static meshes. Skinned meshes are detected but intentionally blocked until bone remapping, skin weights and inverse bind matrices can be transferred safely. VRM export, Spring Bones, colliders and full Skeleton Merge are not implemented yet. Export GLB is a preview/interchange export, not a VRM file.
Browser-based preview. Model files stay in this browser session; they are not uploaded to a server. Three.js and the VRM loader are loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is required.
